# n8n AI receptionist and concierge

An AI-based automated telephone receptionist and concierge system built on the n8n automation platform. It started as my research paper at Dunaújvárosi Egyetem (University of Dunaújváros).

## What it does

- Answers incoming calls with an AI voice agent
- Recognises what the caller wants and routes or answers it
- Books, logs and follows up through n8n workflows
